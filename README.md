# Amazon – Sum-of-the-Parts DCF Model

A DCF valuation of **Amazon (AMZN)** built on an operating model, with separate DCFs for each business segment.

## Build
- **Operating model** with revenue and margin forecasts
- **Segment DCFs** for North America, International and AWS, plus a consolidated DCF
- Unlevered free cash flow: EBIAT + D&A – capex – change in net working capital, with a stub-period discount
- **WACC** sheet (CAPM cost of equity, after-tax cost of debt) and **diluted share count**

## Key assumptions
- WACC **8.3%**, terminal growth **2.5%**
- Revenue forecast to grow about 11% a year, from $575.8 billion (2023) to $784.8 billion (2026)
